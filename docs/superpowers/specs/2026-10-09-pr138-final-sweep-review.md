## Open items (sweep part 2, 2026-10-12: O1, O2, O3 FIXED on this branch at Taher's request; O4 stays)

- **O1 FIXED 2026-10-12.** `PhotoQueue.purgeOrphanedFailures()` (run from `InventoryStore.onListCompleteChanged`) discards `failed` items of the signed-in identity whose row is absent from the COMPLETE list. Tests FU22-FU30. Trade-off accepted: the later purge uses the row-gone check alone (R1's body check governs only the immediate 404), so a local list that reports complete while missing a live product could lose a failed photo. Original finding: An invisible `failed` item stays until sign-out (misrouted endpoint + product deleted elsewhere). Option: purge `failed` items whose row is absent from a complete list. Trade-off: that drops R1's second signal, so a local list glitch could lose a live photo. Taher decides.
- **O2 FIXED 2026-10-12.** New `_isInFlight(photoId)` guard at the top of `_failUpload` and in the new `_completeUpload`; a report for an item that is no longer `uploading` (cleared on sign-out, already resolved) is dropped, so no `photoUploaded` under the next account. Tests FU15, FU18, FU19, FU20, FU21. Original finding: A late 2xx after `PhotoQueue.clear()` (sign-out) emits `photoUploaded` under the next account, and `InventoryStore.applyPhotoIds` matches by `productId` only. Needs an in-flight upload, a sign-out, and the same productId in the next tenant. Pre-existing, not in this diff. Candidate fix: stamp uid/tenantId on the in-flight item and ignore callbacks that no longer match.
- **O3 FIXED 2026-10-12** by the same guard: the first of `onreadystatechange` / `ontimeout` resolves the attempt, the second is a no-op, whatever Qt does. Tests FU13, FU14, FU21. Original finding: On a timeout Qt may fire both `onreadystatechange` (DONE, status 0) and `ontimeout`, counting the breaker twice and rescheduling twice. Unverified. Device or Qt-source check first.
- **O4** The client string and the server literals (`functions/index.js` L1183, `functions/lib/photoCleanup.js` L78) share no source, so a server-side rename makes R1 inert (safe, no data loss). Server tests pin the string.
